# Why Existing Platforms Fail & The Innovation Space

No single platform currently solves this problem entirely. The market is fragmented, with each platform addressing only a small part of the ecosystem. This gap is precisely why a unified national portal is required for SIH.

---

## 1. Existing Platform Breakdown & Limitations

### Campus Placements & TPO Dashboards

* **Examples:** Superset
* **Limitations:** Functions primarily as a CRM for resume submission and shortlist tracking. It lacks skill assessment, personalized skill mapping, and faculty features.

### Internship Portals

* **Examples:** Internshala, AICTE Internship Portal
* **Limitations:** Purely transactional (apply and hire). They do not offer AI-driven skill gap analysis (e.g., showing a student their percentage match for a role) and completely ignore faculty development programs (FDPs) or consultancy.

### Tech Hiring & Assessment Platforms

* **Examples:** HackerRank, Mercer | Mettl, Guvi
* **Limitations:** Used only at the end of the hiring funnel for testing. They lack continuous skill tracking, academic integration, and dynamic portfolio building.

### Continuous Learning Platforms

* **Examples:** Coursera for Campus, LinkedIn Learning
* **Limitations:** Siloed learning data. Progress made on these platforms does not flow into university placement workflows or corporate recruitment pipelines.

### Professional Networks

* **Examples:** LinkedIn
* **Limitations:** Data is self-reported and unverified. Academic institutions (TPOs and Deans) lack structured dashboards to track student skill levels and placement readiness at scale.

---

## 2. Key Innovation Opportunities

1. **Dedicated Faculty Portal:** Build a structured system for academicians to access industry projects, Faculty Development Programs (FDPs), and consultancy roles—a feature currently missing across existing platforms.
2. **Verified Micro-Skill Tracking:** Track granular micro-skills (e.g., Express.js routing, MongoDB aggregation) rather than broad categories (e.g., Web Development) to power accurate role-matching engines.
3. **Unified Data Architecture:** Design a single schema connecting Learning, Assessment, and Placement modules so that completing an assessment automatically updates student portfolios and refreshes job recommendations in real time.
